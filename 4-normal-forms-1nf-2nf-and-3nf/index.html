Module 4

Normal Forms: 1NF, 2NF, and 3NF

This lesson walks through the first three normal forms in detail, providing step-by-step examples of transforming unnormalized tables into 1NF, 2NF, and 3NF. Students will practice identifying and eliminating partial and transitive dependencies.
