COP4708

Relational Databases and Normalization Concepts

This module introduces the foundational principles of relational databases and the normalization process. Learners will explore table structures, keys, functional dependencies, and normalization forms from 1NF through BCNF, building the analytical skills needed to design efficient, anomaly-free database schemas.
