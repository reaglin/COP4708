COP4708

Relational Databases and Normalization Concepts

This module guides you through the foundational principles of relational databases and the normalization process — from understanding how data is organized into tables and keys, to recognizing structural design flaws such as insertion, update, and deletion anomalies, and applying normalization techniques (1NF through BCNF) to build consistent, efficient schemas.

This reading defines and illustrates update anomalies, showing learners how denormalized tables force multiple row updates to reflect a single real-world change and how failing to do so creates data inconsistencies.
