Module 6

Data Modeling and Entity-Relationship Modeling

This lesson introduces data modeling concepts and the Entity-Relationship (ER) model, teaching students how to identify entities, attributes, and relationships in a problem domain. Students will create ER diagrams to visually represent database structure before implementation.
