COP4708

Advanced Normal Forms: BCNF, 4NF, and 5NF

This module extends your understanding of relational database normalization beyond Third Normal Form, examining Boyce–Codd Normal Form (BCNF), Fourth Normal Form (4NF), and Fifth Normal Form (5NF). You will learn to detect violations, compare the trade-offs among normal forms, and apply decomposition strategies to produce well-structured, anomaly-free schemas.
