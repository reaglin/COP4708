COP4708

Advanced Normal Forms: BCNF, 4NF, and 5NF

This module extends your understanding of relational database normalization beyond Third Normal Form. You will explore the limitations of 3NF, learn to detect and resolve Boyce–Codd Normal Form (BCNF) violations, and examine the principles behind higher normal forms — 4NF and 5NF — that address multi-valued and join dependencies in complex schemas.
