Module 7

Designing Relational Databases from ER Diagrams

This lesson covers the systematic process of converting ER diagrams into relational database schemas, including handling one-to-many, many-to-many, and weak entity relationships. Students will practice mapping ER constructs to tables, primary keys, and foreign keys.
