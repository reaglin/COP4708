Pedagogical Purpose

This interactive design board serves as a scaffolded practice and self-assessment tool, giving students immediate visual feedback as they attempt to apply ER-to-relational mapping rules — directly supporting MO1 (identifying correct table structures and key assignments for strong and weak entities), MO2 (placing foreign keys for one-to-many relationships), MO3 (constructing junction tables for many-to-many relationships), and MO4 (forming composite primary keys for weak entities). The scoring and checklist mechanism also supports MO5 by prompting students to evaluate their completed schema for primary key integrity, referential integrity, and normalization against the original ER diagram.
