COP4708

Introduction to Databases, Types, and Applications

This module introduces the core concepts of databases — what they are, how they are structured, and where they are used in the real world. Explore foundational database models including relational, NoSQL, and hierarchical systems, and learn how to compare them to make informed architectural decisions.
