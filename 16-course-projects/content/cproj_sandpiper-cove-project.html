Sandpiper Cove Project

Done by: Group of 4 · Submissions: 4 · Total: 30 points

About the project

This is a group project where the students must design a database system for a veteranarian's office. Students are given office forms used to collect data by the office, interview transcripts with staff and data from the forms,

The scenario

The students may use any database and they will design and build the database, populate it with data and use their system to perform queries and gnerate reports.

What you work from

  • Sandpiper Cove Shelter Forms (Operational Forms Packet) — Forms used in database design
  • Sandpiper Cove Animal Shelter: Staff Interview Summary — Interviews with staff for data collection and requirements
  • Data for Sandpiper Cove Project (opens another site) — Current data collected on paper forms.

Submission 4: Peer Evaluation

Submit as: the form on its page, one for each teammate

Rate each of your teammates (not yourself) on how they took part in this project. Be honest and specific: your ratings are seen only by your instructor, and they count toward each teammate's grade.

Rate each teammate on

  • Did their share of the work, on time
  • Came to meetings prepared
  • Communicated clearly and answered messages
  • Listened to and respected the others' ideas
  • The quality of what they contributed

Scale: 1 = never · 2 = rarely · 3 = sometimes · 4 = usually · 5 = always
