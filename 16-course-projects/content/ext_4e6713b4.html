Sandpiper Cove Shelter Forms (Operational Forms Packet)

Open the file →

Author / Site: COP4708 Course Instructor / Sandpiper Cove Animal Shelter (fictional case scenario)

Summary: This is an interactive HTML packet of operational paperwork for Sandpiper Cove Animal Shelter, a fictional open-admission municipal shelter and adoption center. It presents a stack of numbered, revision-dated business forms — intake, animal records, adoption and office-use sections, data tables, checkbox/option fields, terms and signature blocks — organized with a sticky form index. The forms are supplied explicitly as source material for a COP4708 database design project.

Relevance to Module: The packet serves as the client-supplied documentation that drives the course project: students must read real-world style forms to discover the entities, attributes, data types, required fields, lookup values, and relationships the shelter's database must support. Because the forms also contain reporting artifacts (office-use sections, logs, and tabular records), they indicate the outputs and reports the finished system must produce. It ties together normalization, schema design, forms, and reporting topics surveyed across the course.

Pedagogical Purpose

Instructors include this resource as an authentic primary-source requirements document so students practice eliciting specifications from client paperwork rather than from a pre-written schema — the core skill assessed in MO1 (building a database and reports to meet a set of client specifications). Working from these forms gives students concrete practice identifying tables, keys, constraints, and report requirements, and provides a shared, verifiable basis for grading project deliverables against stated client needs.
