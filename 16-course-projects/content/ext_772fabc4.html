Sandpiper Cove Animal Shelter: Staff Interview Summary

Open the file →

Author / Site: COP4708 Course Project Materials (instructor-prepared client scenario)

Summary: This HTML document compiles requirements-gathering notes from interviews with seven staff members at the fictional Sandpiper Cove Animal Shelter, including the shelter manager, veterinarian, animal care lead, adoption counselor, animal control officer, and foster/volunteer coordinator. Each interview includes role context, direct quotes, business rules, and numbered information requests (R-01 through R-36) stated in the staff's own words rather than as technical specifications. It also frames the project deliverables — a normalized ERD and table definitions, cleaned and loaded data from the clerk's Excel workbook, a query for every request, and a readable results report — and fixes September 30, 2026 as the reporting 'today.'

Relevance to Module: This is the client-voice half of the COP4708 course project: it supplies the business rules and information needs that the shelter's paper forms packet (SC-000 to SC-800) and the messy Excel workbook cannot express on their own. Students must translate these informal, sometimes conflicting staff requests into entities, relationships, constraints, and queries. The 'open questions' and ambiguous wording deliberately mirror the incomplete requirements database designers encounter in practice.

Pedagogical Purpose

The interviews give students an authentic primary source of client specifications so that MO1 — building a database and reports to meet a set of client specifications — is grounded in real stakeholder language rather than a pre-written schema. The numbered requests R-01 to R-36 double as a testable requirements checklist, letting students verify that their design, queries, and final report actually answer what the client asked. Working through the vagueness and the business rules also builds the requirements-analysis judgment that precedes normalization and reporting.
