    Reference File🌐 Web page
    Sandpiper Cove Shelter Forms (Operational Forms Packet)

    This is an interactive HTML packet of operational paperwork for Sandpiper Cove Animal Shelter, a fictional open-admission municipal shelter and adoption center. It presents a stack of numbered, revision-dated business forms — intake, animal records, adoption and office-use sections, data tables, checkbox/option fields, terms and signature blocks — organized with a sticky form index. The forms are supplied explicitly as source material for a COP4708 database design project.

  • 🌐
    Reference File🌐 Web page
    Sandpiper Cove Animal Shelter: Staff Interview Summary

    This HTML document compiles requirements-gathering notes from interviews with seven staff members at the fictional Sandpiper Cove Animal Shelter, including the shelter manager, veterinarian, animal care lead, adoption counselor, animal control officer, and foster/volunteer coordinator. Each interview includes role context, direct quotes, business rules, and numbered information requests (R-01 through R-36) stated in the staff's own words rather than as technical specifications. It also frames the project deliverables — a normalized ERD and table definitions, cleaned and loaded data from the clerk's Excel workbook, a query for every request, and a readable results report — and fixes September 30, 2026 as the reporting 'today.'
