COP4708

Designing Relational Databases from ER Diagrams

This module guides learners through the systematic process of translating Entity–Relationship diagrams into well-structured relational schemas. Topics progress from foundational ER notation and relational concepts through mapping strong entities and attributes to tables, and on to expressing one-to-many relationships — equipping students with a repeatable, principled design workflow.
