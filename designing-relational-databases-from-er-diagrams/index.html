COP4708

Designing Relational Databases from ER Diagrams

This module guides learners through the systematic process of translating Entity-Relationship diagrams into well-structured relational schemas. Topics progress from foundational ER notation and relational concepts, through mapping strong entities and their attributes, to representing one-to-many relationships as foreign keys in relational tables.
