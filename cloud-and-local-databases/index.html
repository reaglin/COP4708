Cloud and Local Databases

This module explores the landscape of modern database deployment, contrasting locally hosted solutions with cloud-based managed services. You will examine infrastructure setup and management responsibilities, and dive into specific platforms such as AWS RDS and Azure Database services to build a practical understanding of how and where data lives.
