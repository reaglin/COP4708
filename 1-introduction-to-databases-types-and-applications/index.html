COP4708

Module 1 — Introduction to Databases, Types, and Applications

This module introduces the foundational concepts of databases — what they are, how they are structured, and where they are used. Learners will explore relational, NoSQL, and hierarchical database models, compare their characteristics, and connect abstract concepts to real-world applications.
