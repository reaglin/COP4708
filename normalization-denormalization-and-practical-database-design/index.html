Normalization, Denormalization, and Practical Database Design

This module guides learners through the theory and practice of relational database design — from first principles of normalization through deliberate denormalization strategies used in production systems. Explore functional dependencies, ER-to-schema translation, and the trade-offs that shape real-world databases.
