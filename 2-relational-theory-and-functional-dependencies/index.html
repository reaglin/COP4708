COP4708 — Module 2

Relational Theory and Functional Dependencies

This module establishes the mathematical foundations of the relational model — sets, domains, tuples, and relations — and introduces functional dependencies as the formal basis for database schema design and normalisation. Work through the readings and practice materials below to build rigorous conceptual and analytical skills before progressing to normalisation theory.
