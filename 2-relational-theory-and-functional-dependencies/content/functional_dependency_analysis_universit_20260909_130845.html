Your task: For each potential functional dependency (FD) listed in the exercise, choose whether it is Valid, Invalid, or Partially Valid based on the sample data. Use the Hint button if you need a nudge. When you are ready, click Check Answers.

Validity Key

Valid – the FD holds for every row in the data with no counterexamples.
Invalid – at least one clear counterexample exists in the data.
Partially Valid – appears to hold in some cases but breaks down in others due to data variation.
